DS Trading

Trading & Sourcing Solutions
China 🇨🇳  →  Zambia 🇿🇲  |  Zimbabwe 🇿🇼

DS Trading is the customer-facing brand of Haikou Duoshao Trading Co., Ltd. (海口多少贸易有限公司), a company registered in the Hainan Free Trade Port, China.

Founded by Zimbabwean and Zambian entrepreneurs based in China, we help individuals, entrepreneurs and businesses access Chinese manufacturers, suppliers and products while simplifying the challenges of sourcing and importing from China.

Whether you need industrial machinery, electronics, solar products, beauty products, event equipment, smart home solutions or custom products, our team is on the ground in China to help identify suitable suppliers, negotiate prices, inspect goods and arrange shipping.

Our Services
✅ Product sourcing & supplier verification
✅ Price negotiation with manufacturers
✅ Product inspection & quality checks
✅ Order consolidation
✅ Export documentation
✅ Shipping & logistics support
✅ On-demand sourcing across a wide range of product categories

We work directly from China and maintain connections in both Zambia and Zimbabwe, allowing us to provide clear communication, regular order updates and support throughout the sourcing and shipping process.

Our Approach
At DS Trading, our goal is simple
You tell us what you need.
We handle the sourcing, procurement and logistics.

Whether you're placing your first small order or sourcing products for an established business, we aim to make purchasing from China clearer, more accessible and easier to manage.
