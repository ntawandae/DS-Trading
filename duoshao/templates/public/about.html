Who We Are

DS Trading is the customer-facing brand of Haikou Duoshao Trading Co., Ltd. (海口多少贸易有限公司), registered in the Hainan Free Trade Port. Founded by Zimbabwean and Zambian entrepreneurs based in China, we help individuals and businesses access Chinese manufacturers and products, hassle-free.

What We Source

Industrial machinery, electronics, solar products, beauty products, event equipment, smart home solutions and custom products — our team is on the ground in China to find suppliers, negotiate prices, inspect goods and arrange shipping.
